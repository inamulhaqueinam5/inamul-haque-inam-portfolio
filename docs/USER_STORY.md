**The Core Problem**
Currently, I lack a centralized portfolio website. As a result, I am unable to present my versatile skill set and projects professionally across both tech and non-tech job domains in one cohesive space.

**Detailed User Story**

* **As a:** Versatile fresh CSE graduate with a strong passion spanning software development, data analytics and corporate leadership.
* **I want to:** Build an eye-catching, dynamic and centralized portfolio website where all my projects and research work are neatly organized into well-defined categories.
* **So that:** I can project myself as a high-value professional, enabling recruiters for roles like Software Engineer, Business Development, Business Development Executive, Web Developer, ML Engineer, Data Scientist, Data Analyst, Management Trainee Officer and Banker to easily evaluate my qualifications and get in touch with me directly through the site.
